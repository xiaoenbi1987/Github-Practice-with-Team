Humpty Dumpty had always loved the view from the top of the wall. Every morning he climbed the crumbling stones of the old castle boundary, wobbling only a little, to watch the sun spill gold across the kingdom. The villagers below whispered that an egg had no business sitting so high, but Humpty simply smiled and waved. What none of them knew was that today, a strange crack had appeared in the wall beneath him, and it was growing.
The crack gave a loud groan, and Humpty felt the stones lurch beneath him. Instead of freezing in fear, he threw himself backward onto the castle side of the wall, landing in a heap on a bale of hay the stable boy had left there that morning. Shaken but perfectly whole, Humpty lay staring at the sky while the section of wall he had been sitting on tumbled down into the empty road below. The villagers rushed over, astonished, and for the first time nobody whispered that an egg didn't belong up high. Humpty laughed, dusted the straw from his shell, and promised to watch the view from the safety of the garden from then on.
