Humpty Dumpty had always loved the view from the top of the wall. Every morning he climbed the crumbling stones of the old castle boundary, wobbling only a little, to watch the sun spill gold across the kingdom. The villagers below whispered that an egg had no business sitting so high, but Humpty simply smiled and waved. What none of them knew was that today, a strange crack had appeared in the wall beneath him, and it was growing.

The wall gave way with a groan, and Humpty tumbled through the morning air into the waiting dust below. The king's horses and the king's men came running, but no amount of trying could put the shattered shell back together. Yet in the quiet that followed, the villagers who had once whispered began to weep, for they realized how much brightness he had brought to their days. From that day on, they left a single golden feather on the wall each sunrise, so that the view Humpty loved would never be empty. And when the light spilled across the kingdom, it seemed, just for a moment, that someone was still up there, waving.
