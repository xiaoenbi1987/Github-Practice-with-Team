Humpty Dumpty had always loved the view from the top of the wall. Every morning he climbed the crumbling stones of the old castle boundary, wobbling only a little, to watch the sun spill gold across the kingdom. The villagers below whispered that an egg had no business sitting so high, but Humpty simply smiled and waved. What none of them knew was that today, a strange crack had appeared in the wall beneath him, and it was growing.
